About The Game

Escape the Glitch is a Roblox obby where reality itself is breaking. Survive strange glitch effects, reach checkpoints, discover secrets, and make your way through the corrupted world.

The game currently supports R15 characters and is still in development.

Game Features

⚡ Glitch Zones

Dangerous areas that can change the way the world behaves.

🌌 Low Gravity

Experience reduced gravity and slow falling.

📺 Screen Distortion

Strange visual effects can distort your screen.

🏃 Speed Boost

Increase your movement speed with the Speed Boost game pass.
