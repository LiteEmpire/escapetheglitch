About

Escape the Glitch is a Roblox experience where reality is breaking apart. Navigate dangerous obstacles, survive glitches, reach checkpoints, and escape before the world completely falls apart.

Features

⚡ Glitch Zones

Enter unstable areas where reality behaves differently.

🌙 Low Gravity

Some glitch zones reduce gravity and change how you move.

🌀 Screen Distortion

Glitches can distort your screen and make navigation harder.

💨 Speed Boost

Use the Speed Boost game pass to move faster through the world.

Progression

🏁 Checkpoints

Reach checkpoints to progress through the experience.

📈 Stages

Your stage tracks how far you have progressed.

💎 GlitchBits

Collect GlitchBits and use them as part of your progression.

🏆 Claim Win

Complete the obby and claim your win at the end.
